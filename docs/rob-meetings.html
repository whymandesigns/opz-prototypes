What is a Rhythm of Business?

A Rhythm of Business (ROB) is the structured cadence of recurring meetings that organizations use to review progress, make decisions, and maintain alignment. Common examples include weekly team standups, bi-weekly leadership syncs, monthly business reviews, and quarterly planning sessions.

When you link an ROB meeting to an Initiative, that initiative automatically appears on the meeting's agenda when it is Active or At Risk. This removes the manual work of deciding what to cover — the system surfaces what needs attention.

ROB meetings live at the org level Meetings are created and managed by admins in the ROB Meetings section of Operationalize. When creating an initiative, you link to existing meetings rather than creating new ones. If a meeting you need doesn't exist yet, ask your admin to create it first.

Linking meetings to an Initiative

1
Open the ROB Meetings tab
Inside the initiative form, navigate to the "ROB Meetings" section. You'll see a list of all ROB meetings configured for your organization.
2
Search or browse meetings
Use the search bar to filter by meeting name, cadence, or owner. Meetings are grouped by team and frequency — weekly, biweekly, monthly.
3
Click "Link" to attach
Linking is immediate. The initiative will appear in that meeting's auto-generated agenda starting from the next occurrence.

How the agenda works

Once linked, Operationalize inserts the initiative into the meeting agenda automatically. The agenda entry includes:

  • Current status (On Track, At Risk, etc.)
  • Owner name and last status update date
  • Any blockers flagged since the last meeting
  • Key Result progress from linked OKRs

Initiatives marked Complete are automatically removed from future agendas. Initiatives marked Draft are not shown until they become Active.

Best practices

Link to the right level A tactical initiative (a sprint goal, a bug fix release) belongs in a team-level weekly. A strategic initiative (a new product line, a market expansion) belongs in an exec review. Linking to both adds noise to the exec forum.
Update status before the meeting The agenda is generated 1 hour before meeting start. If you haven't updated your initiative status since the last meeting, the agenda will show stale data. Build a habit of updating status the morning of your ROB meeting.
