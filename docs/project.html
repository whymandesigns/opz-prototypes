Fields

FieldRequiredDescription
team Required The team that owns this initiative. Determines who receives team-level reporting, which ROB meetings are suggested by default, and which admin has oversight. Teams are configured by your organization admin.
parent_project Optional A parent Project or Program this initiative rolls up into. Use this when your initiative is one piece of a larger program (e.g. "EMEA Expansion" as the parent of "Localization Launch" and "Compliance Certification"). Parent projects aggregate progress across their child initiatives.
contributors Optional Team members who can edit the initiative. Contributors can update status, Key Results, documents, and linked items. They receive edit-related notifications but not ownership-level escalations. Add everyone actively working on the initiative.
visibility Optional Controls who can see this initiative. Defaults to Company-wide. See the Visibility section below for details on each option.

Visibility settings

SettingWho can see itWhen to use
Company-wide All members of your Operationalize workspace. Default for most initiatives. Transparency is usually better — people can't stay aligned on things they can't see.
Team only Members of the owning team plus contributors. Early-stage initiatives that aren't ready to share broadly, or team-internal work that isn't relevant to the wider org.
Private Only the owner and contributors. Sensitive work — HR initiatives, M&A due diligence, executive planning. Initiative will not appear in any shared views or digests.
Start public, go private intentionally The default is Company-wide for a reason. Most initiatives benefit from broad visibility — stakeholders can find context without having to ask, and unexpected connections get surfaced. Only restrict visibility when there's a clear reason to.

Managing contributors

Contributors are added by searching for users within your organization. You can add individuals or entire teams as contributors.

Contributors can:

  • Edit all initiative fields including status, documents, and linked items
  • Update Key Result progress on linked OKRs
  • Post updates and comments on the initiative

Contributors cannot:

  • Transfer ownership (only the owner or an admin can do this)
  • Delete the initiative
  • Change visibility settings
