What is an Initiative?

Initiatives represent meaningful business bets — a product launch, a process improvement, a strategic pivot. Unlike a task or ticket, an Initiative lives at the intersection of strategy and execution. It has a clear owner, a time horizon, and a set of measurable outcomes.

Creating an Initiative in Operationalize automatically creates the connective tissue between your planning tools: it links to your OKRs, surfaces the right recurring meetings, connects your Jira project, and keeps relevant documents in one place.

Creating an Initiative

To create an Initiative, click New Initiative from the Initiatives list or hit C anywhere on the page. You'll be walked through a form with the following sections:

Basic Info
Name, description, owner, dates, and status.
ROB Meetings
Link recurring Rhythm of Business meetings to this initiative.
OKRs
Connect Objectives and Key Results to track measurable outcomes.
Slack Channels
Attach Slack channels where the team communicates about this work.
Jira
Link Jira epics or projects to surface delivery progress.
Documents
Attach PRDs, briefs, specs, and other reference material.
Project
Associate this initiative with a parent project or program.

Next steps

Start with Basic Info to set up the core details, then work through each section to connect your initiative to the tools your team already uses.
